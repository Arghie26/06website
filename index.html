Rizal Lives Again On Stage: UP Revives ‘Noli’ and ‘Fili’ 130 Years After His Death

QUEZON CITY, Philippines. Dulaang Unibersidad ng Pilipinas is staging back-to-back reimaginings of Jose Rizal’s novels, Noli Me Tangere and El Filibusterismo, for its 49th Theatre Season in commemoration of Rizal’s 130th death anniversary. The season opened with Fiestas y Muertos, a reimagining of Noli Me Tangere directed by Jose Estrella, which ran from October 23 to November 15, 2026 at the IBG-KAL Theatre in UP Diliman. It will be followed by Nitrogliserina, a new play based on El Filibusterismo, scheduled for February to March 2027. The productions ask which corrupt structures and illusions must be abolished to achieve real social liberation, echoing the same issues Rizal exposed in the 1880s and 1890s, systemic corruption, abuse of power, and civic apathy. Like in Rizal’s time, the plays use art to awaken Filipinos and remind them that the fight he started through writing continues today.

GOMBURZA Execution in 1872 Sparks Growing Calls for Justice and Reform in the Philippines

MANILA, Philippines. The execution of three Filipino priests, Mariano Gomez, Jose Burgos, and Jacinto Zamora, collectively known as GOMBURZA, on February 17, 1872, marks a pivotal moment in Philippine history. The Spanish colonial authorities charged these priests with complicity in the Cavite Mutiny, leading to their public execution by garrote at Bagumbayan. Their tragic demise ignited a wave of anger among Filipinos, serving as a catalyst for future nationalists, including Dr. Jose Rizal, who honored them through his novel El Filibusterismo. This incident galvanized the burgeoning movement against the injustices of Spanish colonial rule, urging Filipinos to advocate for equal rights and liberty. Today, their legacy serves as a powerful reminder of the values of justice, free expression, and the necessity of safeguarding individuals against wrongful accusations and abuses of authority.

Pax Silica and the Cost of Economic Development

MANILA, Philippines. The Board of Investments (BOI) expects the signing of the proposed Pax Silica artificial intelligence (AI) industrial hub between The Philippines and the United States within the year. The project aims to establish a semiconductor and AI ecosystem in New Clark City, Tarlac. It is expected to consume about 130 million liters of water a day and 3 gigawatts of electricity. Political Dynasty Under Fire: A Modern Reflection of Corruption During Rizal’s time.

MANILA, Philippines — Retired Supreme Court Justice Antonio Carpio warned that the proposed anti-political dynasty bill in the House of Representatives could further enhance political dynasties to higher positions rather than dismantle them, as its limited restrictions would still allow relatives to hold different government positions at the same time. While most lawmakers are opposing the bill as a realistic balance between limiting political power and protecting the people’s right to vote, Carpio’s Dapat Isa Lang Movement argues that the ban should extend to relatives up to the fourth degree to prevent families from monopolizing public office. The issue has gained renewed attention following the Supreme Court’s order for Congress to finally pass an anti-dynasty law, nearly 39 years after the constitutional provision was established. This situation reflects the corruption and abuse of power during Dr. José Rizal’s time, when Spanish colonists, especially friars and higher officials and influential families often used their positions to maintain wealth, privilege, and control over ordinary Filipinos. Just as Rizal exposed the injustices of those who placed personal and family interests above the welfare of the people, the continued dominance of political dynasties raises the question of whether the Philippines has truly moved beyond the corruption and inequality he fought against.

RRizal statue to grace Philippine Embassy in Ottawa, Canada

A life-size statue of Dr. Jose Rizal will be placed at the Sentro Rizal of the Philippine Embassy in Ottawa, Canada.
